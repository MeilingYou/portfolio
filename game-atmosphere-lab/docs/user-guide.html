What is Game Atmosphere Lab?

Game Atmosphere Lab is an educational Progressive Web App (PWA) that teaches you how different video game art styles, colors, typography, and sounds are used to create specific moods and feelings in players.

Each "atmosphere" is based on a real game genre and shows you exactly why it looks and feels the way it does.

Getting Started

1
Open the App Visit the app in your browser or launch it from your home screen if installed.
2
Choose an Atmosphere Click any button in the "Choose an Atmosphere" menu at the top of the page.
3
Explore the Content Read about the art style, mood, and why it creates that atmosphere. Look at the image and notice how colors and visuals match the description.
4
Play the Sound Click "Play Atmosphere Sound" to hear audio that matches the mood. Click again to pause.

App Features

Atmosphere Menu

The row of buttons at the top lets you switch between 8 different game atmospheres. The currently selected atmosphere is highlighted. Each click instantly changes the page's colors, fonts, image, and available audio.

Atmosphere Content

Each atmosphere card shows:

  • Game Examples — real games that use this style
  • Art Style — description of the visual design approach
  • Mood — the emotional feeling it creates
  • Why It Creates This Atmosphere — an explanation of the design choices

Audio Player

The "Play Atmosphere Sound" button plays looping audio matched to each atmosphere. The button label changes to "Pause Atmosphere Sound" while audio is playing. Audio automatically stops when you switch atmospheres.

💡 TIP Some browsers block audio from playing automatically. If sound doesn't start when you switch atmospheres, just click the Play button manually.

Bookmarking & Sharing

Each atmosphere has a unique URL (e.g. #neon-cyberpunk-city). You can copy the URL from your browser's address bar to bookmark or share a specific atmosphere directly.

Installing the App

On Android (Chrome)

  • Open the app in Chrome
  • Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top right
  • Select "Add to Home Screen"
  • Tap "Add" to confirm

On iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  • Open the app in Safari
  • Tap the Share button (□↑) at the bottom
  • Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  • Tap "Add" to confirm

On Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

  • Open the app in Chrome or Edge
  • Look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar
  • Click it and select "Install"
📶 OFFLINE USE Once installed, the app works fully offline. All images, audio, and content are saved to your device on the first visit.

Troubleshooting

Images not showing

Make sure your image files are inside an images/ subfolder in the project directory and are named exactly as listed in data.json.

Audio not playing

Check that your audio files are inside an audio/ subfolder and are in MP3 format. Some browsers require a user interaction (like clicking a button) before audio can play.

App won't install

Make sure you're visiting the site over HTTPS (required for PWA install). GitHub Pages automatically provides HTTPS.
